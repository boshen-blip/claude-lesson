The 10-Point Singapore Used-Car Buying Checklist

Whether you buy from us or anywhere else, run through this before you commit. It's the same checklist we use internally before a car goes on our lot.

  • 1 Check remaining COEYears left drives resale value and renewal cost — factor it into your total budget, not just the sticker price.
  • 2 Verify mileage against ageRoughly 15,000–20,000 km/year is typical in Singapore. Way above or below that is worth asking about.
  • 3 Pull the inspection reportAsk for the latest LTA/VICOM inspection report and any accident history before you commit.
  • 4 Confirm ownership on OneMotoringMatch the plate number to the logbook and check for outstanding loans, summons, or fines.
  • 5 Check for flood or accident damageLook under the carpets and in the engine bay for rust, water lines, or mismatched paint.
  • 6 Take a proper test driveCover highway and stop-start town driving — that's when gearbox and brake issues show up.
  • 7 Compare against market priceCheck similar make, model, year, and mileage elsewhere so you know if the price is fair.
  • 8 Get loan pre-approvalKnow your in-principle approval and monthly instalment before you fall in love with a car.
  • 9 Review the transfer paperworkOwnership transfer, insurance, and any season parking coupons should all be sorted before handover.
  • 10 Ask about after-sales supportEven a used car can come with a short dealer warranty — it's always worth asking.
